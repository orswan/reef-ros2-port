**Odometry revision 1 (VISION §6).** The first scored run (rev. 0, PnP)
failed 3 of 20 items. Raw-frame replay showed PnP mistaking sideways motion
for rotation on the planar wall: lateral −5 %, ascent −20 %, up to 1 m/s
after a loss. Rev. 1 matches 3-D points in both frames' depth and passes
20/20. Its tight inlier threshold relies on exact simulated depth [A].

**Characterizations (not judged).**
- **V1:** the first velocity after an odometry recovery is 1–3 % of the true
  speed (the converter differentiates across the gap).
- **V2:** an intermittent wrong pose just before a depth loss gives a ±0.5
  m/s pair of samples (1 of 3 runs).
- **Closed-loop weak texture** (legacy: no failsafe):
  - the odometry is lost about 13 s into the segment;
  - in 2 of 4 runs it recovered on the return and the vehicle landed near
    its start;
  - in the other 2 it never recovered: REEF σ reached 11.7 m/s, and the
    vehicle drifted into the wall at about 0.38 m/s (−17 m/s²) while REEF
    estimated it was hovering, stayed upright, and landed beside the wall.

**Bugs found and fixed in my own code during P08.**
- The first faults overlay's key did not reach the namespaced node, so the
  hooks never acted, and the analyzer passed the fault items vacuously. The
  analyzer now judges "hook acted" and cross-checks the manifest.
- `camera_check` deserialized every image for the whole run (about 0.6 of a
  core). It now unsubscribes after its check.
- The closed-loop drift metric mixed ENU and NED headings.
- Run manifests now record the actual world and vehicle.
