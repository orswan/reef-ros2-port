## Mechanism

Faults are injected by the scenario runner at a phase start through
**labelled test hooks, off by default** and enabled only by the scenario
overlays (`src/reef_sim/config/closed_loop/*.yaml`): IMU drop in the IMU
adapter (REEF stops producing estimates), range drop, velocity-observation
drop, a graceful exit of the controller (ROS layer; the P06 core is
untouched; respawned by the launch only in that scenario) or of the
stand-in, the estimator's `~/reset`, and a Gazebo pause. Every injection and
its outcome is in `scenario_result.json` (`faults`).

## Findings for R2 and later decisions

- The legacy system has no estimate-freshness or setpoint-freshness check:
  a 6 s stale setpoint keeps the vehicle moving (`setpoint_stale`); any
  command gap above the firmware's 100 ms timeout drops the vehicle in
  simulation (`dropout_long`, `controller_restart`, `standin_exit`).
- A controller process restart cannot be survived: restart time 3.7 s.
- Without velocity observations, REEF's velocity estimate drifts and the
  controller follows it (2.7 m in 10 s); without range, altitude errors of
  0.27 m in 10 s.
- `face_target` makes the heading follow the bearing of a target inside the
  dead zone (heading wander while holding position).
