## Gazebo example world (Apache-2.0)

`src/reef_sim/worlds/x3_flight.sdf` is derived from gz-sim 8.15.0
`examples/worlds/multicopter_velocity_control.sdf` (Open Robotics, Apache
License 2.0). The modifications are listed in its header and in
docs/X3_SCENARIO.md. `x3_closed_loop.sdf`, `x3_vision.sdf` and
`x3_closed_loop_vision.sdf` are generated from it. These files remain under
Apache-2.0; its text is in `src/reef_sim/worlds/LICENSE.Apache-2.0`.
